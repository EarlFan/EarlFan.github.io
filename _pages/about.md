I use computational fluid dynamics (CFD) to study compressible reacting flows, focusing on detonations and how shock waves interact with flames and bubbles. I also develop AI agents that help researchers set up and run CFD simulations.

I am a Research Associate in the High-speed Thermo-fluid and MAV/UAV Lab at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, where I received my PhD in Mechanical Engineering in 2021. [Lab website](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/researchgrp/cywen/index.php/en/index.html)

My open-source projects include [**Fire**](https://github.com/EarlFan/Fire), a CFD solver that uses adaptive mesh refinement to resolve shocks and reaction fronts, and [**ChatCFD**](https://github.com/EarlFan/ChatCFD), an AI agent that turns natural-language requests into OpenFOAM simulation cases.
